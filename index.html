How It Works

  • 1

    Drop Your Tile

    Tap to release a glowing tile onto the moving platform below — timing is everything.

  • 2

    Match the Color

    Hit the same color twice and boom - you'll shoot up higher and get your light back.

  • 3

    Fork the Path

    When the path splits, you've got choices - some need perfect timing, others are just scary as hell.

Features

  • Light Meter Pressure

    Miss your target and you'll lose light fast - gotta stay accurate or you're done.

  • Chain Reaction Combos

    Get three colors in a row and you'll rocket up like crazy - it's the best feeling in the game.

  • Shifting Paths

    The platform never stays still — adapt your rhythm or lose your footing mid-stack.

  • Daily Tower Challenges

    A new path layout every day with a fixed seed, so every player climbs the same stack.
